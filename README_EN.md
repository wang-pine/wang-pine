## 👨‍💻 About Me

I primarily work with **C++ and Go**, while also using Python and TypeScript for engineering tools, automation and rapid prototyping.

My current technical interests include:

* 🎬 **Real-time multimedia and media systems**
* 🌐 **Networking, transport protocols and performance analysis**
* ⚙️ **System architecture, performance optimization and debugging**
* 🤖 **AI Agents, MCP and Agent Runtime**
* 🐧 **Linux and engineering infrastructure**

Rather than simply making a feature work, I'm particularly interested in the behavior behind the system:

> **Why does it work this way? Where is the bottleneck? What happens under failure or load? How can we prove that an optimization actually works?**

I enjoy analyzing problems from the protocol, data-flow, runtime and system-boundary levels, then reducing complex implementations into systems that are simple, reliable, observable and easy to debug.
